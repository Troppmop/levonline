# Lev LaChayal — How to Use the Website

A simple guide to everything you can do on the site, organized by who you are. If you're not sure which section applies to you, start with "Everyone" below.

---

## Everyone

### Signing in

Go to the site and enter your email and password. If you're on your phone, the site is designed to work like a regular app — you can add it to your home screen:

- **iPhone**: open the site in Safari, tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen."
- **Android**: open the site in Chrome, tap the menu (⋮), then "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App."

After that, it opens like any other app icon, no browser bar.

### Your Profile

Wherever you land after logging in, click your name in the top corner (or in the header on mobile) to reach **My Profile**. From there you can:

- Update your name or email
- Change your password (you'll need to enter your current password first)

Changing your password automatically signs you out of any other device you were logged into elsewhere — so if you ever think someone else might have access to your account, changing your password is the fastest way to lock them out.

### Signing out

Click "Sign out," visible in the same place as your name.

## For Residents (Soldiers)

Residents don't manage anything about the house — this is your personal corner of the site to stay in the loop and speak up when something needs attention.

### Getting an account

If you don't have a login yet, click **"Register here"** from the sign-in page. Fill in your name, email, phone, and choose a password, then submit. A staff member reviews every application — you'll be able to sign in with the email and password you chose as soon as they approve it. (There's no notification sent — if it's been a while, just check back or ask a staff member.)

### Your Home screen

After signing in, you'll land on your Home screen, which shows:

- **Your status**: a big button showing whether you're marked Home or Away, with one tap to switch it.
- **The announcement feed**: house announcements, Tefillah times, and anything your host family has posted specifically for you.

### Reporting a problem

Tap **Report** at the bottom of the screen. Choose whether it's a **Maintenance/Damage** issue or a **Cleaning Request**, describe what's wrong, optionally pick which room it's in, and submit. You can attach a photo right from your camera — either when you first report it, or by adding one later to a report you already submitted. You'll see all your past reports and their current status (New, Acknowledged, In Progress, Closed) on the same screen.

### Meal invitations

Tap **Meals** at the bottom to see any invitations your host family has sent you. Each one shows who's inviting you and when — tap **Accept** or **Decline**. Once you've responded, it moves into your history below so you can always see what you said yes or no to.

## For Host Families (Av/Eim Bayit)

Your role centers on the soldiers assigned to you — hosting them, staying in touch, and letting them know what's happening.

### Logging a meal you hosted

Go to **Meals**. Fill in the host family name, who you hosted, and the date, then submit. This builds a running record of your hosting history, visible in the summary alongside everyone else's.

### Inviting a soldier to a meal

On the same **Meals** page, under "Invite Your Assigned Soldiers," pick one of your assigned residents from the dropdown, fill in the date and meal type (Shabbat, Yom Tov, or weekday), and send. They'll see it on their end and can accept or decline — you'll see their response show up next to the invitation.

### Posting to your group

Go to **Announcements**. Anything you post here goes only to the residents assigned to you (plus they'll also see general house-wide announcements posted by staff). Give it a title and a short message, and post.

### Seeing who's assigned to you

The **Soldiers** tab shows the residents paired with your family. You can view their basic info, but editing their profile is a staff/admin job.

## For Staff

You handle the day-to-day running of the house: who's where, what's stocked, what's broken, and what everyone needs to know.

### Dashboard

Your landing page after signing in. At a glance: how many residents are home vs. total, how many inventory items are running low, and how many maintenance tickets are still open. Tap any number to jump straight to that section.

### Presence board

Shows every resident grouped by floor and room, in the same layout as the building itself — not an alphabetical list. Tap a resident's status badge to flip them between Home and Away.

### Managing residents

The **Residents** page lists everyone active in the house. Click **Edit** next to a resident to:

- Assign or change their room
- Update their security deposit amount and mark it paid
- Deactivate them (for a move-out — this doesn't delete their history, just marks them inactive and drops them off the active list)

To add a brand-new resident yourself (rather than waiting for them to self-register), use the form at the top of the page.

### Managing rooms

The **Rooms** page is where you set up the physical layout — floor number, room number, and capacity. This list is what drives the Presence board's layout, so it's worth keeping accurate.

### Food inventory

The **Inventory** page tracks everything by location (Floor 1/2/3 kitchen, or basement) and category. You can:

- Add a new item with a starting quantity and a low-stock threshold
- Use the **+1 / -1** buttons for quick day-to-day adjustments
- Edit the low-stock threshold directly in the table
- Delete an item that's no longer tracked

Anything at or below its threshold shows up highlighted here and on the Dashboard.

### Maintenance & cleaning tickets

The **Maintenance** page shows every report, whether it came from a resident or was logged by staff. Each ticket moves through **New → Acknowledged → In Progress → Closed** — click **Advance** to move it to the next stage. You can't skip stages, which keeps the history meaningful for anyone checking back later.

### Meal coordination

The **Meals** page shows the full hosting log and summary across every host family, plus lets you log a hosting or send an invitation yourself if you're helping coordinate.

### Announcements

Post house-wide announcements — general notices, Tefillah time updates, or emergencies — visible to every resident. You can pin important ones to the top of everyone's feed.

### Reviewing new sign-ups

The **Registrations** page lists every resident application. For each pending one, you can:

- Optionally assign them a room right away
- **Approve** — this creates their resident profile and activates their login in one step, using the password they chose when they applied
- **Reject** — optionally with a short note explaining why; no account gets created

Switch between the Pending / Approved / Rejected tabs to see the full history.

## For Administrators

Everything Staff can do, plus account management for the whole system.

### Creating accounts

The **Staff** page is where you create logins for anyone who needs one:

- **Staff** or **Admin** accounts — full name, email, temporary password, done.
- **Av/Eim Bayit** accounts — same, just pick that role.
- **Resident logins** — pick "Resident" as the role, then choose which existing resident profile the login belongs to from the dropdown. (You'd use this if you want to set someone up directly rather than having them self-register.)

### Everything else

Administrators see the same Dashboard, Presence board, Residents, Rooms, Inventory, Maintenance, Meals, Announcements, and Registrations pages as Staff — with nothing hidden or restricted.

## Good to know

- **Passwords need to be at least 8 characters.**
- If you enter the wrong password too many times in a short window, the site will briefly stop accepting login attempts as a security measure — just wait a minute and try again.
- Nothing you do gets lost partway through — if something goes wrong while saving, the site backs out the whole change rather than leaving things half-done.
- The site works offline in the sense that it loads instantly even on a weak connection, but anything you *do* (reporting an issue, changing a status, sending an invitation) still needs an internet connection to actually save.
